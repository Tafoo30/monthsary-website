A NOTE FROM ME TO YOU

To my love,

Sometimes, I find myself wondering how one person can mean so much to me. How one person can be so many things at once. How someone can be my comfort, my happiness, my warmth, my peace, and sometimes, the very person who makes me want to pull my hair out when we're arguing.(WHAHAHAHAHA KA CORNY PERO MWA). But somehow, even with all those things, you remain my favorite person in this world. And I don't think words will ever be enough to explain just how much you mean to me, baba. You are such a beautiful person babuu, inside and out. And when I say beautiful, I don't mean just the kind of beauty that catches someone's eyes. I mean the kind that stays with someone, the kind that makes a person want to know you more, understand you more, and love you in all the ways you deserve to be loved. You have such a soft and gentle soul, such a big heart, and a way of caring that makes people feel seen and understood. You're an empath, someone who feels deeply, someone who carries so much in that heart of yours. And I hope you know how precious that is. I hope the world never makes you believe that your softness is something you need to hide, because to me, it is one of the most beautiful things about you, my baba Jirah. I love how I can be serious with you, how we can talk about life and the things that weigh on our minds, reflect on ourselves, and open up about the things we don't always know how to explain. I love that I can have deep conversations with you and still, a few moments later, be laughing with you over the most ridiculous things. I love our playful banter, our little jokes, the way we can tease each other, and how you can be both my person for the deepest conversations and my partner in the silliest moments. With you, I don't have to choose between being serious and being childish. I can be both. I can be every version of myself, and somehow, you still feel like the person I want beside me, baba. And there's something about being in your embrace that makes me feel so small in the most comforting way. Like a little child who has finally found somewhere safe to rest. For a moment, I don't have to be strong, I don't have to figure everything out, and I don't have to pretend that I have everything under control. I can simply exist in your arms, feel your warmth, and let the world be quiet for a while. You make me feel like I can put down all the things I've been carrying, even if only for a little while. And I don't know if you realize how special that is to me. To find someone who makes you feel safe enough to be soft, especially in a world that can be so exhausting, is something I will never take for granted. You are my home in ways I never expected another person could be. Not a place made of walls or a roof, but a feeling. A warmth that reaches me even on my coldest days. A blessing that came into my life and made ordinary things feel a little more meaningful. When life becomes too much, when my thoughts become too loud, or when I simply need someone to talk to, you are one of the people I want to turn to. You remind me that there is still gentleness in this world, that there are still people worth trusting with the most vulnerable parts of ourselves, and that love can feel like resting after a long, tiring day. And I'm so thankful that I have you gaa. And, of course, I cannot forget that you also have your dragon mode moments. (di nako mo dungag WAHHAAH). My soft-hearted, gentle girl who can suddenly become the fiercest creature alive when we're arguing or not in good terms. Sometimes, you really test my patience, and I'm sure I test yours, too. But even then, you're still you. You're still the girl I adore, the girl whose feelings matter to me, the girl I want to understand even when we're having a hard time understanding each other. Loving you doesn't mean I think you're perfect or that every moment between us will be easy. It means I love the whole of you, your softness, your strength, your moods, your laughter, your stubbornness, and all the little things that make you the person I fell in love with, jirah. If there's one thing I hope you never forget, it's that you don't have to do anything extraordinary just to deserve my love. You don't have to be happy all the time, strong all the time, or have everything figured out. You don't have to prove that you're worthy of being cared for. You are already someone worth loving simply because you are you. And if I could give you one thing, aside from all the love I have for you, it would be the ability to see yourself through my eyes, even just for a moment. Maybe then, you'd understand why I feel so lucky to have you, why I admire you so much, and why my heart continues to find its way back to you, baba. Because, my love, you are so much more than someone I love. You are someone I am grateful to know, grateful to hold, grateful to laugh with, and grateful to share this life with. And every day that I get to call you mine is another day I find a new reason to be thankful for you.

Always yours ♡
